# Compass landing page — version A vs. version B

Two takes on the Compass landing page, built to be reviewed side by side. On the `homepage-version-b` branch both are deployed together: **A at `/compass-a`**, **B at `/compass-b`**, and the Compass nav dropdown lists both so a reviewer can flip between them without leaving the site (`/compass` resolves to B). Nav, footer, tokens and every other page are identical; only the landing page differs.

| | **A** (`main` · `/compass-a`) | **B** (`homepage-version-b` · `/compass-b`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The bet | Meet the visitor where they are, then explain the method | Show the visitor what they'll be holding at the end, then work backward |
| Written for | Someone already provisioned: primary CTA is *Open my Compass* | An Engine lead who hasn't booked yet: primary CTA is *Book a kickoff*; *Open my Compass* is a small secondary link |
| Hero | Dark deep-blue art, promise headline, two CTAs | Ultramarine art, the final document mocked up as a stack of pages beside the headline |
| Interactive element | Mood picker (four entry states → tailored first step) | Three-step viewer (kickoff call · self-paced in My Compass · synthesis call → one sentence on what happens, one line on what you leave with) |
| How the method is explained | Three layers (Industry → Ecosystem → Engine) as cards | As the table of contents of the final document |
| Outcomes | Four text cards | Three document mocks, headings only (configuration summary, the integrated diagnostic document, the finalized three-voice version) |
| The ask | FAQ only | No timeframes anywhere — they vary by Engine; the FAQ says so |
| The Gem | Its own dark section with three cards | Never named: B says "AI-powered data analytics", one of three role cards beside your team and the navigator + strategist |
| Closing | Ends on FAQ | Centred deep-blue band, same art treatment as the hero: *Book a kickoff* |
| Cohort references | None | None — program-general by design; every mock uses bracketed blanks, no Engine named |

## What B responds to in the notes

- "Landing page should state concretely what users will get out of it" (Aug 6) → the deliverable is the hero, and every step is shown as its output.
- Section review 1.3: "the single most persuasive thing we could show — a page of a real output — isn't here yet" → the deliverable mocked up in the hero and in the viewer, program-general.
- Section review 1.4: "two mental models on one screen (layers *and* milestones)" → one spine: three steps; the five milestones are a list inside step 2, and the layers appear only as the final document's sections.
- "Tease the experience, connect Engines to Ryan, preview milestones" (Aug 19) → the viewer previews the shape of the work; the closing band books the kickoff.
- Section review 1.1: "Open your Compass assumes the user is signed in" → B assumes the opposite and books a kickoff.

## What B gives up

- The mood picker. B speaks to one state (deciding whether to do this) and lets the FAQ carry the "obligated" and "curious" objections.
- The dedicated Gem section, and the word "Gem" itself. B calls it AI-powered data analytics and gives it a third of one section; the Learn topics still use the Gem name.
- Nothing is tailored per visitor; B is the same page for everyone.

## Questions to put to reviewers

1. Five-second test on each hero: what does this do, who is it for, what would you click?
2. Does the viewer get used, and do people open the two call steps or stay on the deliverable?
3. Do the bracketed blanks read as "this will be about us" or as "this is a template"?
4. With no timeframes on the page, do people ask how long it takes before they book — and is the FAQ answer enough?
5. Which page would you send to an Engine lead you've never spoken to? Which to one who's already said yes?
