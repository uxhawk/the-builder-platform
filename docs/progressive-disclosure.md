# Progressive disclosure model

Principle from the Aug 19 discussion: **revealed complexity** — savvy users click through quickly; hesitant users expand for more context. Nothing is hidden, but nothing is dumped.

## Four levels

| Level | What you see | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 — Orientation | One sentence on what the Compass is, what it takes, your first step given your state | Landing hero + mood picker |
| 1 — The shape | Seven milestones, one line each, status at a glance | Landing overview strip · portal stepper · collapsed milestone heads |
| 2 — The work | What you leave with · time · artifact · three columns (Prepare / In the Gem / Reflect) · paste-able prompt · questions for your team · off-ramp · complete | Expanded milestone card |
| 3 — The why | Frameworks, taxonomies, data caveats, personas | "Go deeper" chips → Learn library; sidebar disclosures (hypothesis history, configuration) |

## Rules

1. **Current milestone opens by default**; everything else is one click away. Locked milestones expand to a *preview* (what you'll do, what you'll leave with) so nothing is a surprise, but they are not actionable.
2. **Framework vocabulary is second**. Cards use plain language ("why hasn't someone already fixed it?"); the taxonomy names live in Learn and in the Gem prompt.
3. **Archetypes are teased, not led with**. They appear in Learn and in the 02/04 prompts as hypotheses.
4. **One hard gate, visibly explained**: 04 → navigator review → 05. The notice says why (nothing reaches the team unchallenged).
5. **Help is ambient**: floating "Get help", a flag on every card, a people card in the sidebar. Off-ramp copy is milestone-specific ("this is where a thought partner helps most").
6. **The Gem is one click and one paste away** from any actionable milestone; the page never pretends to be the Gem.
7. **State is legible**: stepper, status pills, artifact list, hypothesis version, deadline countdown — all answer "where am I, what's next".
